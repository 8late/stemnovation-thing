🔺 Math: Shapes

Shapes are all around us. Look at the things near you and see what shapes you can spot.

Remember: A triangle has 3 sides. A square has 4 equal sides.
Can you find a circle?

A clock face or a wheel may look like a circle. Look around your room!

➕ Math: Numbers

Numbers help us count toys, snacks, books, and so much more.

Example: 2 apples + 1 apple = 3 apples.
You have 3 blocks and get 2 more. How many now?

Count 3, then 4, 5. You have 5 blocks!

✍️ English: Verbs

A verb is a word that can tell what someone or something does.

Examples: jump, read, draw, laugh.
Find the verb: “The rabbit hops.”

“Hops” is the verb because it tells what the rabbit does.

📚 English: Nouns

A noun names a person, place, animal, or thing.

Examples: teacher, park, kitten, pencil.
Which word is a noun: “happy” or “flower”?

“Flower” is a noun because it names a thing that grows.

💨 English: Adverbs

An adverb can tell us more about an action, such as how it happens.

Example: “The turtle walks slowly.” Slowly tells how it walks.
Which word tells how Mia sings?

In “Mia sings sweetly,” the word “sweetly” tells how she sings.

🧩 English: Sentences

A sentence shares a complete thought. Word order helps the sentence make sense.

Example: “The little dog runs.”
Which sounds right: “Birds can fly” or “Fly can birds”?

“Birds can fly” is the usual word order for this sentence.
